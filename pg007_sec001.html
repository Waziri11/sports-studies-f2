Chapter One

Netball

Introduction

Netball is a team sport where players use their hands to pass, and shoot the ball into the opponents’ goal to score goals. It is played by male and female competitively and recreationally. Engaging in netball provides employment opportunities, enhances physical health, and helps players develop essential life skills. In this chapter, you will learn the concept, safety measures, skills, and rules of netball. The competencies developed will enable you to analyse and play netball skillfully and confidently.

Concept of netball

Netball is a team sport played by hands on a rectangular court with clearly defined areas. A team consists of seven (7) players, each assigned with specific roles and position that restricts movement. The players must pass the ball to their teammates within three (3) seconds of receiving it, aiming to move the ball and score towards their attacking goal. The game is played in four quarters, each lasting for 15 minutes with four (4) minutes break between the first-second and third-fourth quarters, and an interval of 12 minutes at the half time.

A team scores when the Goal Shooter (GS) or Goal Attack (GA) successfully shoots the ball through the opponent’s hoop. At the end of the match, the team with the highest number of goals is declared the winner. If the game ends in a tie, extra time or other tie-breaking rules may be applied depending on the competition’s regulations. Netball promotes teamwork, discipline, and strategic thinking while improving physical fitness, hand-eye coordination, and spatial awareness. It is inclusive, allowing participation regardless of skill level, gender or ability, making it a valuable activity for recreation and competition.
