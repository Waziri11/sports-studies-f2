Skills and techniques in playing netball

The primary objective of netball is to score goals by shooting the ball through the opponent’s goal ring while preventing the opposing team from scoring. To play netball effectively, players need to develop key skills and techniques that enhance their performance on the court. Techniques are the parts that make up a whole skill. The skills include passing, movement, shooting, positioning and defending, all of which contribute to the overall performance of the player, consequently, to the success of the team.

Passing

Passing is one of the most important skills in netball as players are not allowed to run with the ball. Effective passing ensures smooth ball movement, maintains possession, and facilitates teamwork. There are several types of passes that players can use to move the ball around the court, each serving a particular purpose depending on the situation. The common types of passes include chest pass, bounce pass, overhead pass, and shoulder pass.

Chest pass

A chest pass is a two-handed pass delivered from the chest for a fast, accurate delivery over short distances. It is most effective when defender pressure is low and is ideal for quick exchanges between teammates.
