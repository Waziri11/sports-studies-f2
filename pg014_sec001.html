  1. (i) Stand with legs shoulder-width apart, slightly bending your knees;
  2. (ii) Hold the ball with both hands or one hand at chest height, positioning your elbows out in front;
  3. (iii) Spread your fingers on either side of the ball, with thumbs behind it and fingers pointing downward;
  4. (iv) Step forward with your dominant foot as you release the ball at wrist height, pushing it forward toward the ground, as shown in Figure 1.7;
  5. (v) Aim for the ball to bounce just over halfway between you and the receiver; and
  6. (vi) Follow through by extending your arms upon releasing the ball to ensure accuracy and keep the ball on the right path.
