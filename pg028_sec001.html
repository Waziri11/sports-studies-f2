Positioning

In netball, each player has a specific position with defined roles and movement restrictions on the court. Understanding positioning is crucial for effective teamwork, strategy, and gameplay. The seven playing positions in a netball team are divided into attacking, defending, and midcourt roles. Each position plays a vital role in maintaining team structure, ensuring smooth ball movement, and defending the opposing team.

Attacking positions

These are the positions between the centre circle and the goal third of the attacking team including the goal circle. It covers the area played by the Goal Shooter (GS), Goal Attack (GA) and Wing Attack (WA).

(i)

Goal Shooter (GS): Primarily responsible for scoring goals. Positioned inside the goal circle, they work closely with the Goal Attack (GA) to create shooting opportunities;

(ii)

Goal Attack (GA): Assists the Goal Shooter (GS) by feeding the ball into the goal circle and taking shots when necessary. GA plays in the centre third, and attacking goal third including the goal circle; and

(iii)

Wing Attack (WA): Plays a role to deliver the ball to the shooters (GS & GA). Focuses on accurate passing, positioning, and creating space in the attacking third.

Midcourt position

Centre (C): This is the position between defense and attack position. The Centre dictates the speed of the play, breaks up opposing attacks and keeps possession of the ball. They also link the play from defenders to attackers, often creating chances for forward players. The centre moves across all thirds except the goal circles of both teams. The centre starts and restarts the play after each time the goal is scored.
