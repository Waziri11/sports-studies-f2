Defensive positions

This is the position between the centre circle and the goal third of the defending team including the goal circle. It covers the area played by the Wing Defense (WD), Goal Defense (GD) and Goal Keeper (GK). Players playing in the defensive positions have the responsibility to prevent the opposing team from scoring.

  • (i)
    Wing Defense (WD): Responsible for marking the opposing team’s Wing Attack and preventing them from feeding the ball into the shooting circle;
  • (ii)
    Goal Defense (GD): Marks the Goal Attack, preventing them from taking shots and intercepting passes into the goal circle. They also assist in transitioning the ball to attack; and
  • (iii)
    Goal Keeper (GK): The last line of defense, responsible for marking the Goal Shooter and preventing goals. They stay in the defensive third and inside the goal circle.
Table 1.1. Description of players’ playing positions and their roles
Player(s) Playing position Roles
Goal Shooter (GS) Attacking goal third, including the goal circle
  • Scores goals.
  • Takes rebounds.
  • Competes for the ball against the Goal Keeper (GK).
Goal Attack (GA) Centre third and attacking goal third, including the goal circle
  • Scores goals.
  • Takes rebounds.
  • Takes free passes inside the goal circle.
Wing Attack (WA) Centre third and attacking goal third
  • Feeds the ball into the shooting circle.
  • Supports the shooters.
Centre (C) All thirds except goal circles
  • Links defense to attack.
  • Takes centre passes.
