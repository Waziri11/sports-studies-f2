Wing Defense (WD)

Centre third and defensive goal third
  • Marks the Wing Attack (WA).
  • Prevents passes into the goal circle.
  • Intercepts passes.

Goal Defense (GD)

Centre third and defensive goal third, including the goal circle
  • Marks the Goal Attack (GA)
  • Blocks and intercepts passes.
  • Defends against shots.
Goal Keeper (GK)

Defensive goal third, including the goal circle
  • Marks the Goal Shooter (GS).
  • Defends against shots.
  • Takes rebounds.

Rules and regulations for playing netball

The netball rules ensure fair play, safety, and consistency across all levels of competition. The rules are updated periodically, however, they remain rooted in core values such as equal opportunity, fair play, and respect for opponents. Below are the key rules governing the game:

1. Team composition

A netball team consists of seven players on the court and each player has specific position that determines where they can move on the court. Players must play within their designated areas to maintain the structure and prevent unfair advantages. The player’s positions include Goal Shooter (GS), Goal Attack (GA), Wing Attack (WA), Centre (C), Wing Defense (WD), Goal Defense (GD), and Goal Keeper (GK).

2. Match duration

A standard netball match is played over four quarters, each lasting for 15 minutes. There are short breaks of not more than four minutes after the first and third quarters
