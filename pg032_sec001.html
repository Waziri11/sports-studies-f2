8. Footwork

A player must not take more than one step while holding the ball. If a player lands on one foot when catching the ball, can pivot but must release the ball before lifting the landing foot. If a player lands on both feet simultaneously, may choose one foot to pivot while keeping the other grounded. Taking extra steps results in a footwork infringement, causing possession to be handed to the opposing team.

9. Throw-ins and free passes

When the ball goes out of court, the team that did not touch the ball last is awarded a throw-in which is taken from the point where the ball crossed the boundary line. The player taking the throw-in must have at least one foot close to and touching the line, and must release the ball within three seconds. A free pass is awarded for minor infractions, such as offside and over-a-third violations or footwork errors. The player taking the pass must stand still before throwing the ball to a teammate.

10. Penalties and sanctions

A penalty in netball is a sanction awarded for serious rule infringements. For instance, a penalty pass is given when a player commits a major foul, such as obstruction or contact. Meanwhile, a sanction refers to the action taken by an umpire in response to an infringement committed by a player, team official, or bench player. The penalised player must stand out of play until the pass is taken. Additionally, if a player holds the ball for more than three seconds, the opposing team is awarded a free pass for a “held ball” violation. Umpires may also issue warnings or suspensions for repeated rule-breaking or unsporting behaviour.
