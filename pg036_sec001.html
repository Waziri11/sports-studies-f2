Chapter Two

Field Events

Introduction

Field events is a sport focusing on jumping and throwing activities. It is performed by male and female individuals for recreation or competition. Engaging in field events provides employment opportunities, enhances physical health, and helps players to develop essential life skills. In this chapter, you will learn the concept, safety measures, skills, and rules of field events. The competencies developed will enable you to analyze and perform the various field events skillfully and confidently.

Concept of field events

Field events is a sport that encompasses athletic activities conducted on the field as opposed to track events. The activities are classified into two categories: throwing events and jumping events. The jumping events include long jump, triple jump, high jump, and pole vault while throwing events consist of the shot put, discus throw, and javelin throw. These events can be organised as competitive sport or recreational activities, and are integral to major athletic competitions such as the Olympics, World Championships, and regional meets. When organised as a competition, field events assess an athlete’s strength, technique, and coordination, highlighting their ability to propel themselves or an object across a measured distance.
