Shot placement

Placement is the positioning of the shot near the shot putter’s chin. An athlete positions the shot under the jawline, close to the neck. The following steps will help you execute a shot placement:

  • (i) Rest the shot comfortably at the base of the fingers;
  • (ii) Press the shot against the hollow base of the neck, just beneath the jaw;
  • (iii) Keep your palm facing upward and your thumb pointing downward; and
