Discus throw

Discus throw is a field event in which an athlete throws a heavy, plate-shaped implement (the discus) from within a marked circle to as far a distance as possible. The winner is a thrower who throws a discus to the longest distance starting from the throwing circle.

Facilities and equipment

Discus throwing requires facilities such as the throwing area and measurement tools. It also requires some equipment including the discus, shoes, chalk, stopwatch, flags (white and red), and recording sheet.
