(b) Throwing the discus

Throwing the discus is the propelling of the implement in the proper direction with correct body movements. It starts from the discus thrower’s stance to the release of the discus. The following steps will help you throw the discus:

(i)
Place the discus in your throwing hand with your fingers spread evenly along the rim;
(ii)
Stand at the back of the throwing circle with your feet shoulder-width apart, facing away from the direction of the throw;
(iii)
Rotate your upper body while extending the discus arm backwards, keeping your non-throwing arm extended forward for balance;
(iv)
Turn your body by shifting weight onto your dominant leg, engaging your core to generate rotational momentum while staying low and balanced;
(v)
Plant your non-dominant foot first as you near the front of the circle, pulling your throwing hand back, ready to release;
(vi)
Extend your throwing arm forward while rotating your wrist for a smooth spin, aiming for a high, flat trajectory for maximum distance, Figure 2.16; and
(vii)
Continue the motion forward after release to maintain balance, keeping your eyes on the discus, and stay in the circle until the discus lands.
