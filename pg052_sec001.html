Rules and regulations for discus throw

The following are some rules and regulations for discus throw:

  • 1. Men’s and women’s discus shall be 2 kg and 22 cm, and 1 kg and 18 cm in weight and diameter respectively;
  • 2. The thrower shall commence throw from a stationary position within the circle and may place the discus inside the circle or outside the circle;
  • 3. The thrower shall not leave the circle till the discus has landed in the sector;
  • 4. The chief judge shall control the whole event supported by two judges; one for checking legal throws, the other judge shall act as a recorder, in-charge of throwers and standards; and
  • 5. All discus throws shall be within the cage enclosure for the safety of the participants.

Jumping events

Jumping in athletics involves athletes propelling themselves off the ground to achieve a maximum distance or height.

There are four main jumping events in athletics: long jump, triple jump, high jump, and pole vault.

These events are categorised into horizontal jumps, which focus on covering distance, and vertical jumps, which focus on height.
