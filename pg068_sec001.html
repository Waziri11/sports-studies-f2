Vertical jumps

Vertical jumps include the high jump and pole vault. These jumps require the athlete to jump as high as possible from the ground into a landing surface normally cushioned with sawdust, rice hull or mattresses.

High jump

The high jump is one of the field events where athletes attempt to jump high as much as possible to clear the bar. Each jumper has three trials to clear the bar. Depending on the event, the bar is raised for 3.8 or 5.1 cm for the next height after all jumpers have tried to clear the prior reading. Only those who cleared the previous height qualify for the new height. This process continues until the last jumper misses to clear the bar after making three trials.

Equipment and facilities

High jump requires facilities and equipment such as the jumping area, and a variety of equipment including tape measures, marking devices and maintenance tools.

Jumping area

The jumping area is comprised of the semi-circular runway with a radius of either 15 m or 20 m and a landing area, Figure 2.33. The runway allows an approach run for 15 m from any angle within a 180-degree arc. The runway should have a similar level to the court.
