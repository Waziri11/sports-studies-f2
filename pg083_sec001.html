Goggles

Goggles are protective eyewear designed to protect the eyes from water, chlorine, salt, and other irritants during swimming. They also provide clear vision, allowing swimmers to maintain focus, especially during long swims or races. Goggles are typically made from soft and adjustable materials like rubber seals to ensure a watertight fit around the eyes, Figure 3.4.

Swim cap

A swim cap is a close-fitting, flexible head covering worn while swimming, Figure 3.5. The cap helps to protect hair, and reduce drag, which can slow down performance. It is made of materials that offer a snug fit that protects the hair from chlorine damage. In competitive swimming, the swim cap also serves to streamline the swimmer’s head and reduce drag, allowing for faster speed in the water.
