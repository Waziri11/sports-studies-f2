Precautionary measures in swimming

Swimming is an enjoyable and beneficial activity, but it requires adherence to safety measures to prevent accidents, injuries, or drowning. Observe the following precautionary measures to ensure a safe and enjoyable swimming experience.

(a) Warm-up before swimming

Performing light stretching and warm-up exercises before entering the water helps prevent muscle cramps, enhance flexibility, and reduce the risk of injuries while swimming.

(b) Swim in designated areas

Always swim in areas that are supervised by lifeguards, such as designated swimming pools or marked sections of open waters. Avoid swimming in unknown or restricted areas such as areas with strong currents or deep waters.

(c) Know your swimming ability

Swimmers should be aware of their skill level before taking part in swimming.

(d) Supervision for children and beginners

Children and inexperienced swimmers should always be supervised.

(e) Observe pool and open water rules

Follow all posted signs and safety rules in swimming facilities. For example, avoiding to: run on slippery surfaces, dive into shallow water, or engage in rough play that may lead to injuries.

(f) Use proper swimming equipment

Wearing appropriate swimming gear such as swimsuits, goggles, and swim caps ensures comfort and safety in swimming. Beginners should use flotation devices like kickboards or life jackets for added safety.
