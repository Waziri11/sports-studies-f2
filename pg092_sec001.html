Kicking

Kicking is a fundamental swimming skill that generates propulsion and helps maintain body position in the water. Effective kicking improves speed, balance, and efficiency in different swimming strokes. Each stroke requires a specific kicking technique to maximize movement and maintain coordination with the arms and breathing.

Flutter kick

Flutter kick is one of the most commonly used kicking techniques in swimming. It consists of small, rapid, alternating leg movements. This kick is used in front crawl and backstroke to propel the swimmer forward while keeping the body aligned, Figure 3.14.
