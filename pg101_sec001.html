2.

Stroke and movement

(i) Swimmers may use any stroke, but the front crawl is the most commonly used stroke; and
(ii) Any forward motion is allowed as long as it does not violate other competition rules.
3.

Turn and wall contact

(i) In races with multiple laps, swimmers must touch the wall before turning; and
(ii) Flip turns (tumble turns) are commonly used for faster transitions.
4.

Breathing and head position

(i) Swimmers can breathe as often as they want, usually turning their heads to the side; and
(ii) The head may be submerged, but swimmers must surface within 15 m after the start and each turn.
5.

Disqualification

(i) A swimmer who moves before the starting signal may be disqualified for a false start;
(ii) Swimmers who fail to remain in their designated lanes and interfere with others are disqualified;
(iii) Failure to touch the wall at the end of a lap or the finish also results in disqualification; and
(iv) Staying underwater beyond 15 metres after the start or a turn.
