(iii) Swimmers can stay underwater for up to 15 metres after the start but must surface before passing that mark.

2. Stroke and body position

(i) Swimmers must remain on their back throughout the race, except when performing a turn;
(ii) Any arm movement is allowed, but alternating arm strokes and a continuous flutter kick are typically used; and
(iii) The swimmer’s body must be positioned flat on the back; though slight body rotation is allowed.

3. Turning

(i) Swimmers must touch the wall with any part of their body when turning;
(ii) A swimmer is allowed to rotate onto their stomach during the turn but must complete the turn quickly; and
(iii) After pushing off from the wall, swimmers may remain underwater for up to 15 metres before surfacing.

4. Finishing

(i) The race ends when a swimmer touches the wall while still on their back; and
(ii) Swimmers cannot turn onto their front before touching the wall.

5. Disqualification

A swimmer may be disqualified for:
(i) Not remaining on their back (except during the turn);
(ii) Performing an illegal turn or failing to touch the wall;
(iii) Staying underwater beyond 15 metres after the start or a turn; and
(iv) False starting (leaving before the start signal).
