(iii) The head must break the water’s surface before completing the second arm stroke.

2. Stroke and body position

  • (i) Swimmers must stay on their stomachs at all times; turning onto their backs is not allowed;
  • (ii) Both arms must move at the same time in a circular, scooping motion;
  • (iii) The legs must perform a frog kick (breaststroke kick) where the feet turn outward and sweep back together;
  • (iv) Alternating or flutter kicks are not allowed and can lead to disqualification; and
  • (v) After each stroke, the head must break the water surface.

3. Turning

  • (i) Both hands must touch the wall simultaneously at each turn;
  • (ii) A swimmer can briefly dive underwater after pushing off but must surface before starting the second arm stroke; and
  • (iii) A single pull and kick are allowed after pushing off the wall before resuming normal strokes.

4. Finishing

  • (i) Swimmers must touch the wall with both hands at the same time at the finish; and
  • (ii) The body must remain in a prone (face-down) position when finishing the race.

5. Disqualification

A swimmer may be disqualified for:

  • (i) Using a wrong kick, such as a flutter or dolphin kick;
  • (ii) Failing to touch the wall with both hands simultaneously at turns and finish;
  • (iii) Performing asynchronous arm movements (arms must move together);
  • (iv) Failing to break the surface of the water after the second stroke; and
  • (v) Turning onto the back at any point during the race.

Practice a breaststroke swimming race while adhering to its rules.
