Butterfly swimming race

The butterfly swimming requires the swimmer to swim using butterfly stroke throughout the race. Below are the key rules governing butterfly swimming race:

  1. 1.

    Starting

    • (i) The race begins with a dive start from the starting block; and
    • (ii) After entering the water, swimmers are allowed one arm pull and up to two dolphin kicks underwater before surfacing.
    • (iii) Swimmers must break the surface of the water before completing the second arm stroke.
  2. 2.

    Stroke and body position

    • (i) Swimmers must stay on their stomach (face-down position) throughout the race. Turning onto the back is not allowed;
    • (ii) The arms must move simultaneously and symmetrically in an overwater recovery motion;
    • (iii) The legs must perform a dolphin kick, where both feet move together in a wave-like motion; and
    • (iv) Any form of flutter or breaststroke kicking is not allowed.
  3. 3.

    Breathing and head position

    • (i) Swimmers may breathe at any time, but they must lift their head forward while keeping their body streamlined; and
    • (ii) The head must remain facing forward or downward when not taking a breath.
  4. 4.

    Turning

    • (i) Both hands must touch the wall simultaneously at each turn;
    • (ii) After turning, swimmers may perform one arm pull and up to two dolphin kicks underwater before resurfacing; and
    • (iii) The swimmer must resume a regular stroke pattern before reaching 15 m from the wall.
  5. 5.

    Finishing

    • (i) The race ends when a swimmer touches the wall with both hands simultaneously; and
    • (ii) The body must remain in a prone (face-down) position when finishing.
  6. 6.

    Disqualification

    A swimmer may be disqualified for:

    • (i) Failing to touch the wall with both hands simultaneously at turns and finish;
    • (ii) Performing different arm movements or leg kicks other than that of a butterfly;
