(iii) Failing to break the water’s surface within 15 metres after the start or a turn; and

(iv) Turning onto the back at any point during the race.

Medley swimming race

Medley refers to a race that involves multiple strokes, either performed by one swimmer (individual medley) or by a team (medley relay).

The rules that govern medley are the same which govern the various strokes used in the race.

In these races, a swimmer starts with butterfly, then backstroke, breaststroke and lastly front crawl.

In a medley relay, four swimmers each swim one stroke in the following order: backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly, and finally front crawl.
