Gymnastics

Introduction

Gymnastics is a sport that involves a variety of physical exercises and routines performed on different apparatuses. It is practiced by both males and females for competitive and recreational purposes. Participating in gymnastics promotes physical health, creates employment opportunities, and helps individuals to develop essential life skills. In this chapter, you will learn the concept, safety measures, skills and rules in gymnastics. The competencies developed will enable you to analyse and perform a variety of gymnastics activities with confidence and proficiency.

Concept of gymnastics

Gymnastics is both a competitive and developmental sport that involves performing a variety of movements using apparatuses such as floor, balance beam, vault, rings, parallel bars, and pommel horse. Its main goal is to develop and showcase refined motor skills, physical control, and aesthetic movement. The sport is typically divided into several distinct categories, including artistic gymnastics, rhythmic gymnastics, trampoline, acrobatic gymnastics, and aerobic gymnastics, each with its own technical requirements and performance standards. Gymnastics plays a crucial role in the holistic development of individuals, particularly during early childhood and adolescence. It contributes significantly to the enhancement of fundamental movement patterns, spatial awareness, muscular development, and self-discipline.
