Precautionary measures in performing gymnastics

To ensure safety practice and competition, gymnasts must take a range of precautionary measures to protect themselves and others from injuries. The measures are grouped into three main categories; namely safety gear, safe practices and competition and healthy gymnastics.

Safe gymnastics gear

Using the right equipment is essential for injury prevention and optimal performance. Gymnastics gear falls into the following categories:

(i)
Apparel: This includes properly fitted clothing such as leotards, grips, and wrist guards that allow freedom of movement while minimising risk;
(ii)
Protective equipment: Gymnastics shoes, knee, elbow and ankle supports, and mouth guards help reduce the impact of falls and repetitive strain;
(iii)
Safety equipment: Items like mats, padding, spring floors, spotting blocks, and other training aids provide cushioning and support during practice; and
(iv)
Gymnast-specific gear: These are personalised items that enhance comfort and performance, such as headbands, hair ties, and compression wear.

Safe gymnastic practice and competition

Safety during gymnastic training and competition is maintained through adhering to guidelines, correct execution of techniques/skills, and proactive measures to prevent injuries. For safe gymnastic practices and competition perform the following key safety practices:

(i)
Beginning with proper warm-up and ending with cool-down exercises;
(ii)
Progressing from simple to complex skills to build confidence and reduce the risk of injury;
(iii)
Training under qualified supervision and using spotting techniques;
(iv)
Staying hydrated, following a nutritious diet, and ensuring adequate rest and recovery; and
(v)
Complying with rules and regulations, medically prepared, demonstrating sportsmanship, and maintaining mental readiness.
