Backward roll

A backward roll is a gymnastic skill where a gymnast rolls backward over their shoulders and returns to a standing or seated position. It helps develop core strength, coordination, and body control, making it an essential skill for advanced moves like back handsprings and back tucks. The following steps will help you practice backward rolls:

  1. (i)Stand, then squat with feet together keeping arms raised and chin inserted towards the chest to protect the neck;
  2. (ii)Sit down and roll onto your back, bending your knees toward your chest;
  3. (iii)Place hands by your ears, palms facing upward and push them against the floor to lift the back over the head;
  4. (iv)Roll onto feet and stand up smoothly keeping arms raised for balance, Figure 4.2; and
  5. (v)Repeat the practice several times to perfect the skill.
