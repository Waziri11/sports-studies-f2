Bridge

The bridge is a gymnastics skill that involves arching the back while supporting the body with the hands and feet on the ground. It helps develop flexibility, strength, and control, especially in the spine, shoulders, and wrists. For progressive learning in bridge gymnastics, start with the basic bridge position, followed by the wall bridge before performing the full bridge. The following steps will help you practice the bridge:

Basic bridge position

(i)
Lie on your back, bending the knees while keeping your feet flat and hip-width apart;
(ii)
Place your hands flat on the ground near your ears, fingers pointing toward your shoulders;
(iii)
Push through your hands and feet to lift your body off the ground;
(iv)
Hold for a few seconds, then lower down slowly; and
(v)
Repeat it to acquire a comfortable positioning.
