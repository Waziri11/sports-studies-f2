Gymnastics routines

Gymnastics routines are planned sequences of movements performed by a gymnast on the apparatus.

Each routine is designed to develop the gymnast’s strength, flexibility, coordination, control, balance, and artistic expression.

The type of equipment used determines the nature of the movements, the skills performed, and the judging criteria.

The gymnastics routines include floor routines and equipment based routines.

Floor routines

A floor routine is a choreographed sequence of acrobatic and dance elements performed on a floor in gymnastics.

Headstand

A headstand is a basic inverted balance skill where a gymnast supports their body weight on their head and hands while keeping their body straight and controlled.

It is often used as a beginner skill to develop balance, strength, and body control before progressing to more advanced inversions like handstands.

The following steps will help you practice headstand:

  • (i) Kneel and place your hands shoulder-width apart on the floor;
  • (ii) Create a triangle base with your head and hands for stability;
