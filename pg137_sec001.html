Vault dismount

The vault itself is the dismount from the vaulting table. After completing the vault, ensure that you land properly to show control. The following steps will help you practice it:

(i)
Push off the vaulting table with force, using your arms to propel yourself into the air;
(ii)
Perform any required flips or twists (depending on the vault style); and
(iii)
Spot your landing area then land with both feet simultaneously, keeping your knees slightly bent to absorb the impact, Figure 4.26.
