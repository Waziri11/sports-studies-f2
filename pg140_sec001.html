Cast

Cast is a fundamental skill performed on the uneven bars or the parallel bars and high bars.

It involves lifting the body from a front support position to a higher position, often in preparation for more advanced skills.

There are four main types of cast:

  • (i) Basic cast: The gymnast pushes away from the bar and lifts their body upward from a front support position;
  • (ii) Cast to handstand: The gymnast continues the movement until they reach a fully extended handstand;
  • (iii) Straddle cast: Performed by spreading the legs apart (straddle) while lifting into a handstand; and
  • (iv) Cast to kip: A movement that creates momentum to move into a kip, a common bar skill used in routines.

The cast helps develop shoulder, arm, and core strength leading to more advanced bar skills like giants and releases.

The skill is used in transitions and as a connecting element in a bar routine.

Practicing a basic cast in gymnastics, especially on uneven bars or parallel bars, requires proper technique, strength, and body control.

The following steps will help you practice cast:

  • (i) Begin in a front support position on the bar (arms straight, body tight, and hips against the bar);
  • (ii) Your hands should grip the bar firmly with straight arms and shoulders slightly over the bar;
  • (iii) Lean forward slightly and push through your shoulders, creating momentum;
  • (iv) Engage your core and glutes to keep your body straight;
  • (v) Swing your legs slightly backwards and then kick them forward while squeezing your core.
