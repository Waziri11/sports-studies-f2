(b)

Back support: A position on parallel bars, where gymnast facing away from the bars, holding the body in a straight position with arms extended behind. Follow these steps to practice the back support hold on the bar;

(i)
Stand between the bars at an appropriate height;
(ii)
Place your hands on the bar(s) with your fingers pointing forward;
(iii)
Use a firm grip to support your body weight;
(iv)
Push through your hands and extend your arms fully to raise your hips off the ground;
(v)
Position your body so that it is straight and facing upward, with the chest open;
(vi)
Keep your arms straight and shoulders pressed down and back;
(vii)
Maintain a straight line from shoulders to heels;
(viii)
Look slightly forward or upward to maintain neck alignment;
(ix)
Keep your body in a tight, straight position, Figure 4.38(b); and
(x)
Hold for the required time (e.g., 5–15 seconds).
