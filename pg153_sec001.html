Figure 4.38(b): Back support bars holding

Arabesque hold

A position where the gymnast holds the body in a straight line, balancing on one leg, and lifting the other leg behind them in a horizontal position on the bar. Follow these steps to execute the back arabesque hold:

  1. (i) Stand tall with feet together and arms at your sides;
  2. (ii) Focus your eyes forward to help maintain balance;
  3. (iii) Shift your weight onto your supporting leg;
  4. (iv) Engage your core for stability;
  5. (v) Slowly lift the opposite leg straight behind you, Figure 4.39;
  6. (vi) Point the toes of the raised leg;
  7. (vii) Extend the same arm as the supporting leg forward;
  8. (viii) Extend the opposite arm backward or to the side;
  9. (ix) Keep your body in a straight line and hold the position (5–10 seconds); and
  10. (x) Lower your leg gently and return to the starting position.
