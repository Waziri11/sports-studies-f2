Glossary

Approach run: A running movement toward the take-off board that builds speed and momentum for the jump.

Attacking positions: Positional roles in netball focusing on moving the ball closer to the opponent’s goal circle and creating scoring opportunities. It includes Goal Shooter (GS), Goal Attack (GA), and Wing Attack (WA).

Attacking third of a team: The section of the court closest to the opponent’s goalpost, where a team attempts to score.

Back handspring: A skill where the gymnast jumps backward, flips onto hands, and pushes off to land on their feet.

Backward rolls: A gymnastic movement where the gymnast rolls backward over the shoulders and returns to a standing or seated position.

Balance beam: A narrow beam used in gymnastics for balance exercises.

Bib: Sleeveless vest worn by netball players to identify their positions on the court.

Blocking shots: A defensive skill where players prevent an opponent’s shot from reaching the goalpost.

Bounce pass: A two-handed pass where the ball bounces once on the ground before reaching a teammate.

Bridge: A basic gymnastics skill that involves arching the back while supporting the body with the hands and feet on the ground.

Buoyancy: The ability to float or stay afloat in water.

Cartwheel: A fundamental skill involving sideways movement in a hand-hand-foot-foot pattern, resembling the spokes of a wheel.

Cast: A fundamental skill performed on bars, involving lifting the body from a support position into an elevated position.

Centre circle: A small circle located at the centre of the netball court where play begins and restarts after each goal.

Centre pass: The method of starting or restarting play from the centre circle after a goal is scored.

Centre third: The middle third of the netball court used mainly for transitioning the ball from defense to attack.
