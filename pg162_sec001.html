  • Chest pass: A two-handed pass thrown directly from the chest to a teammate chest-height level.
  • Competitive pools: Pools built according to international standards for professional swimming competitions.
  • Contact: Physical interference (pushing, hitting, or holding) that disrupts an opponent’s play, leading to a penalty.
  • Court: The rectangular playing area marked into thirds used for netball matches.
  • Defending: Actions aimed at preventing the opposing team from scoring by intercepting, blocking, or marking.
  • Defensive third: The section of the netball court closest to a team’s own goalpost, where they defend against the opposing team’s attacks.
  • Dismounts: The gymnastics skills where the gymnast exits the apparatus landing on the floor in a controlled manner.
  • Dodging: Quick, lateral movements to escape defenders and create space for passing or shooting.
  • Endurance: The ability to sustain a workout for extended period of time.
  • First aid kit: A collection of basic medical supplies available at the playing venue for treating minor injuries.
  • Flutter kick: A rapid, alternating leg movement used in front crawl and treading water.
  • Footwork rule: A rule that restricts netball players from taking more than one step with the ball or from dragging the landing foot.
  • Foul jump: A jump where the athlete either steps over the take-off board or lands outside the designated area.
  • Frog kick: A wide, sweeping leg motion used in breaststroke swimming and treading water.
  • Goal circle: A semi-circular area around each goalpost where only designated players are allowed to shoot for a goal.
  • Goalpost: A vertical pole with a horizontal ring and net at the top, used as the scoring target.
  • Goggles: Protective eyewear worn during swimming to protect the eyes and provide clear vision.
