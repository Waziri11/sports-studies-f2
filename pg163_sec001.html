  • Gripping powder: A powder used to absorb sweat and improve grip on apparatuses like the rings and bars.
  • Hand paddles: Flat, rigid devices worn on the hands to increase resistance and improve arm strength.
  • Hand-eye coordination: The ability to move the hands accurately based on visual input, important for catching and passing.
  • Handspring on Vault: A vault skill where the gymnast sprints, jumps onto the springboard, places hands on the vault table, and pushes off to lift into the air.
  • Handstand: A gymnastics skill in which the gymnast supports the entire body on their hands on an apparatus.
  • Hangs: Gymnastics skill where a gymnast suspends from bars or rings.
  • Held ball: A violation that occurs when a player holds the ball for more than three seconds without passing or shooting.
  • Hop: The first jump in the triple jump, where the athlete takes off from one foot and lands on the same foot.
  • Horizontal jumps: An athletic movement where an individual jump forward through the air covering as much as horizontal distance as possible before landing.
  • Intercepting: Gaining possession of the ball by cutting off a pass between opposing players.
  • Jump shot: A shooting technique where the player jumps and releases the ball while at the highest point.
  • Landing: The moment where a player makes contact with the ground after receiving the ball either by jumping or moving to a position to catch the ball.
  • Landing area: The area where the athlete lands, usually filled with sand in jumping events.
  • Landing position: The posture and alignment the athlete adopts when landing, ensuring a controlled finish.
  • Landing sector: A marked area on field event where the thrown implement is expected to land.
  • Lay-up shot: A close-range shooting technique performed while jumping off with one foot near the goalpost.
