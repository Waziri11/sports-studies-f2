Rings: A gymnastics apparatus used in men’s gymnastics for strength-based routines like holds, swings, and dismounts.

Round-off: A gymnastics skill that generates momentum for tumbling moves, involving a two-foot landing and a body rotation.

Runway: The path athletes use to build momentum before take-off in horizontal jumping events.

Safety gear: Equipment designed to protect gymnasts from injuries, including apparel, wrist guards, shoes, and mats.

Sanction: A penalty or corrective action imposed by the umpire after a rule violation.

Scissors leap: A jump where the gymnast kicks one leg forward while the other leg moves backward, creating a scissor-like motion in the air.

Scoring: Achieving a goal by successfully shooting the ball through the hoop from within the goal circle.

Short course pools: Pools that are 25 metres long, typically used for national and club-level competitions.

Shoulder pass: A strong, one-handed pass thrown from the shoulder, suitable for long-range passing.

Speed in swimming: The rate at which a swimmer moves through the water.

Split leap: A jumping skill where the gymnast extends both legs into a split position while airborne, maintaining flexibility and control.

Splits: A flexibility exercise in gymnastics that involves splitting the legs apart in different directions, aiding flexibility and strength.

Springs: Movements involving explosive power and rapid extension of the body to propel the gymnast into the air.

Stride technique: A flight style where the athlete maintains the take-off position until landing during jump event.

Supervision: The act of overseeing swimmers, especially beginners and children, to ensure safety.

Swings: A rhythmic and controlled movement in which a gymnast moves back and forth in an arching motion while hanging from the apparatus such as horizontal bar, rings or parallel bars.
