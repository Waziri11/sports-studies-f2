Sport Studies for Secondary Schools: Student's Book Form Two

  • Take-off angle : The angle at which the athlete takes off from the board, influencing jump distance or height.
  • Take-off board: A rectangular board that mark a take-off point for long jump and triple jump.
  • Take-off foot: The foot used for jumping off the ground during take-off.
  • Technique: A way of performing a specific skills using proper form, control and precision to achieve the best possible outcome.
  • Throwing area: An area in which the javelin throw takes place, including the runway, throwing arc, and landing sector.
  • Umpire: The official responsible for enforcing the rules and ensuring fair play during the netball match.
  • Vault: A gymnastics apparatus used for performing a running leap and somersaults.
  • Vertical jump: An explosive movement in which an individual propels their body upward in a vertical direction from a stationary or while in motion, with the primary objective of achieving maximum height.
  • Wind gauge: A tool used to measure wind speed during jumping events.
